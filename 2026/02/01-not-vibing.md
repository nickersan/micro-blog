# Not Vibing!

Unless you've been living under a rock, you have to have noticed AI and the hype surrounding it.  Soon AI will be curing cancer, exploring theoretical physics and essentially doing
many of the jobs currently performed by us mere mortals.

One such job is mine, computer programming, where the latest craze is what's known as vibe coding - instead of writing code, the programmer simply tells an AI it wants a program 
that does x.  The AI then generates code for you, which if you've any sense, you review, but in the most extreme interpretation of vibing, you just commit.

While admittedly bias, I think the vibe coding approach, as I will explain, is fundamentally flawed.

Computer languages are precise.  The computer does exactly what the programmer tells it (even when what they ask for is wrong).  English is imprecise and ambiguous, as anyone whose 
ever had to read a legal document can attest.  When writing a program, there's typically a dialog between a customer and the programmer (often with additional people in the chain) 
regarding what the program should do - this imprecise dialog is turned into precise instructions.  This dialog is two-way, the customer often doesn't know what they want and part 
of the programmer's job is to explore and clarify the requirements.  

With vibing that conversation remains in the imprecise and ambiguous world of English.

And here's the rub, the thought process involved in exploring the problem, such that, the imprecise English is turned into the precise instructions of a computer language 
necessitates the programmer fully think through the problem being solved - it's nuanced, but with vibing that necessity is missing.
